# Planning without an AI forecast

The practical question behind the forecasting discussion was how to plan a life. Resolving the date of an AI breakthrough need not be a prerequisite for choosing what to do next.

Working approach: choose a near-term direction that remains worthwhile across several futures, then reassess when evidence changes. Develop durable judgement, learn to use and check current tools, and preserve room to change direction. These are aims rather than guarantees of career security.

The scale of a possible historical change does not determine its relevance to today's decision. A new forecast matters when it changes an action; otherwise [[Information Diet|waiting for clearer evidence]] may be enough.

Outside work, [[Capability should direct learning|personally valued capabilities]] and relationships need not depend on outperforming AI. Do not postpone living until the uncertainty is resolved.
