# Rawness and a specific voice

Working hypothesis: part of artistic rawness is the feeling that a particular person had something to express from a particular situation. Voice, place, constraints, and exposure give the work a reason to exist beyond being well formed. The chat uses The Streets' *Original Pirate Material* as its example.

Music can convey this through delivery as well as words: breath, strain, timing, and timbre can carry feeling without an explicit explanation. Imperfection may contribute, but rough production alone does not establish sincerity.

This adds a distinction to [[LLMs]]: producing a convincing expression and having a personal reason to express it are different questions. A model can imitate a specific voice; that does not give it the biography behind that voice. Equally, this distinction does not prove that generated work cannot be original or moving.

**Question to retain:** what did this work articulate that had previously gone unsaid? Recognisable emotional language is only part of that test.
