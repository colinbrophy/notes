# A recurring social experiment

In the chat, I wondered whether my hobbies were too solitary and described feeling neither especially happy nor sad. That is a reflection to explore, not an explanation of its cause.

Solo interests can occupy time without supplying shared experiences or something to look forward to. The proposed correction was to add regular contact rather than discard those interests.

**Suggested experiment, not yet a commitment:** try one recurring social activity each week, ideally with the same people, for a month or two. Afterwards, ask whether there is more anticipation, connection, or enjoyment. The test is whether it helps, not whether I have become a sufficiently social person.

A question to retain: what am I actually looking forward to?
